A rule-based converter from European Portuguese to Barranquenho. The rules come from the *Convenção Ortográfica do
Barranquenho* and the *Gramática Básica de Barranquenho* (Universidade de Évora, 2025). No native speaker has reviewed
the output.

Source code, rules and tests: [TigreGotico/pt2barranquenho](https://github.com/TigreGotico/pt2barranquenho).
