Português → Barranquenho

Barranquenho is the language of Barrancos, on the Portuguese border with Spain. This page converts European Portuguese into Barranquenho with hand-written rules from the Convenção Ortográfica do Barranquenho and the Gramática Básica de Barranquenho (Gonçalves, Navas, Correia; Universidade de Évora, 2025).

The output follows the spelling and grammar rules of the two books. It is not a native translation: no speaker has reviewed it, and Barranquenho has words and turns of phrase that rules cannot predict. Each line is converted on its own, in your browser.
